# Redesign Existing Projects

Redesigns, restyles and reviews of existing pages and app screens follow **design-taste-frontend**, auditing the existing page in a real browser first, keeping its content and working behaviour, and then rebuilding the design to a chosen direction with before-and-after screenshots.

Follow the `design-taste-frontend` skill (its "Redesign or review" workflow):
1. Audit the current state (DOM structure, tokens, layout, typography, accessibility).
2. Establish the target design direction and palette tokens.
3. Apply surgical design upgrades while preserving business logic and responsiveness.
4. Verify visually with browser inspections.
